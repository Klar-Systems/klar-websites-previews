About Us

Thailand Comes to Lapland

Thaibaan Ravintola is Rovaniemi's standout Thai restaurant — a gem for anyone seeking authentic, mouthwatering Thai flavours in northern Finland. Located on the second floor of Kauppakeskus Rinteenkulma on Koskikatu, we bring vibrant, spicy, and fresh flavours of Thailand right to your table.

From aromatic curries and classic Pad Thai to fragrant Tom Yum soups and fresh spring rolls, every dish is prepared with care and authentic ingredients. Our staff are happy to guide you through the menu and accommodate dietary preferences — including vegan adaptations on request.
