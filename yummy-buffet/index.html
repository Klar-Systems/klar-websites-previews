The room

Second floor, Kaisaniemenkatu 2.

Yummy Buffet sits on the second floor of the Fennia-kortteli block in central Helsinki — three minutes' walk from the Central station, one minute from Finnkino Kaisaniemi. Take the stairs up, the dining room opens out across the floor.

"A spacious, peaceful place to eat with a generous variety of dishes. Great sushi, dumplings and it has a salad and dessert bar too."— Wanderlog reviewer

It's a sit-down room, not a takeaway counter — solo lunches at the front, larger groups at the back. Sushi line, hot Asian mains, Thai curry, salad table and dessert bar all under one roof.
