About us

Restaurant Jufu is a beloved spot in Espoon keskus (Entresse shopping center) bringing fresh Asian cuisine to Espoo. With a focus on quality ingredients and authentic preparation, we offer both all-you-can-eat buffet and à la carte options. Whether you're looking for a quick lunch or a full dinner experience, Jufu delivers consistent, delicious Asian food with a welcoming atmosphere.
