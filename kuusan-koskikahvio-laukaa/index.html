On the rapids since the season starts

Kuusan Koskikahvio is the summer café and grill on the rapids in Kuusa — terraces over the water, a guest harbour for boaters, karaoke evenings inside, and grill food that locals have come back for since the place opened. Open from spring through late autumn, with a steady rhythm of events when the weather warms.

Three hundred and fifty reviews and a 4.2-star average from boaters, hikers, and locals who treat this as their summer kitchen. The rapids have been here a long time. So have we.

What’s on

Karaoke nights, live music evenings, and the regular boating-season buzz from spring opening through autumn close. The terrace fills up fast on warm Saturdays — it's that kind of place.

Karaoke evenings

Saturday-night staple. Locals, summer visitors, and the boating crew take turns at the mic.

Live performers

Acoustic acts and seasonal performers through the summer months — check our Facebook for the next date.

Boater's stop

Tie up at the guest harbour and walk straight to the terrace. Coffee, grill food, drinks — and a bench in the sun.
