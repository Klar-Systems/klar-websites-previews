Our Story

Pietarsaari's rock pub, since 2014

Black Sheep Pub & Terrace has been Pietarsaari's go-to spot for live music, cold beer, and a genuinely friendly atmosphere since opening on Isokatu in 2014. We sit right in the centre of the city, with a cosy pub interior and one of the town's best-kept secrets: an inner-yard summer terrace.

We keep things simple — good music, good company, a full bar and a pool table. Whether you stop in for a quiet pint or stay for the band, you are welcome here.

"A great place to have a beer — good music, good atmosphere and a full bar. All you can ask for is right there." — Google review
What's On

Live music and more, all year round

From rock bands and jazz nights to private gatherings, something is always on at Black Sheep. Here is what makes us Pietarsaari's most full-spirited local.

Live Music

Rock, jazz and everything in between — we host live performances year-round. Past acts include Bob Malmström, Marco Luponero & The Loud Ones, and Erik Sjøholm. Follow us on Facebook for upcoming gig dates.

Summer Terrace

Our inner-yard terrace is Pietarsaari's favourite warm-weather spot. Sheltered, relaxed and right in the heart of the city — the perfect place to enjoy a beer when the Finnish summer arrives.

Pool Table

Challenge your mates to a game of pool any time we are open. The table is ready — all you need to bring is your best shot.

Private Events & Groups

Planning a work night out, birthday, or other group event? Black Sheep is a natural setting. Get in touch to discuss arrangements — we are happy to accommodate your group.

Drinks

A full bar, done right

We keep a well-stocked bar — beer on tap, spirits, cocktails and non-alcoholic options. Student discount on beer applies. Ask the bar staff what's pouring tonight.

Full drinks menu available at the bar. Prices and selection may vary.
