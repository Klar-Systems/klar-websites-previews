About the cafe

A second living room in the city centre

Ihana Kahvila Baari sits at Aleksanterinkatu 7-9 — one of Helsinki's busiest pedestrian streets — yet the moment you step through the door the city fades. Plush sofas, warm light, and a genuine personal character make it feel less like a cafe and more like a cherished living room.

Born as a sister to the original Ihana container cafe in Sompasaari, this Aleksanterinkatu outpost brings the same spirit of warmth and inclusion to the heart of the city. Everyone is welcome — students from the nearby University of Helsinki, city regulars, tourists, remote workers, and four-legged friends alike.

With 4.5 stars from 710 Google reviews, Ihana has built a devoted following on honest quality: precise Italian espresso, freshly baked pastries, a thoughtful vegan menu, and the kind of friendly service that never feels intrusive.

Live music & special evenings

Jazz nights at Ihana

Ihana Kahvila Baari is one of Helsinki's intimate jazz venues — a living room with a piano, where musicians and listeners share the same close quarters.

Jazz

Live Jazz Evenings

Local and visiting jazz musicians perform in our intimate space. Past events have featured Warren / Mustonen / Kaariainen and rotating ensembles from the Helsinki jazz community. Follow us on Instagram or Facebook for upcoming dates.

Seasonal

Seasonal Special Menus

We rotate our pastry and drinks menu with the seasons — expect warming spiced drinks in autumn, fresh berry specials in summer, and festive bakes in winter. Check our social channels for what is on this month.

Groups

Private & Group Evenings

Planning a small gathering, team event, or cultural evening? We welcome enquiries for private use of the space — reach out via the contact details below or through our social media.

Stay up to date
