About

Authentic Chinese Buffet in Zeppelin

Lucky Dragon has been serving authentic Chinese cuisine in Kempele's Zeppelin shopping centre since 2011. Located conveniently at Zeppeliinintie 1, the restaurant offers a generous all-day buffet with a wide selection of traditional Chinese dishes.

Known for large portions, friendly service, and good value for money, Lucky Dragon has built a loyal following among Kempele locals and shoppers alike. The buffet features everything from spring rolls and fried rice to specialty meatballs and stir-fried dishes.

The Buffet

Chinese Buffet

A generous spread of traditional Chinese dishes, freshly prepared every day. Salad bar, warm dishes, and a variety of meat and vegetable preparations.

Starters

Spring rolls, fried chicken wings, and Chinese-style meatballs — a spicy specialty rarely found elsewhere.

Main Dishes

Sour pork with mushrooms, spicy chicken, beef and vegetable stir-fries, and more. Generous meat-to-vegetable ratios.

Rice & Noodles

Fried rice, fried noodles, and steamed rice. Classic Chinese staples prepared fresh for the buffet.

Salad Bar

Fresh salad bar where you assemble your own combinations. A variety of vegetables and dressings to complement the warm dishes.
