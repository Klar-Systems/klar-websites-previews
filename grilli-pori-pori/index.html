The Story

Pori's favourite grill — handmade since 1987

Grilli-Pori has been feeding the city from Juhana Herttuankatu since 1987. No franchise, no shortcuts — every burger is made by hand, every portion is sized like it means something.

Thursday and Friday nights the kitchen runs until 04:30. That is not an accident. When Pori's nightlife winds down, Grilli-Pori is still going. It has been the city's last stop for almost four decades.
