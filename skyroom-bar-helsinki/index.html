About Skyroom Bar

A view unlike any other in Helsinki

Skyroom Bar sits on the 16th floor of Clarion Hotel Helsinki, wrapping guests in floor-to-ceiling glass that frames the West Harbour, city rooftops, and — on a clear evening — the horizon all the way out to sea.

The interior is Nordic chic: clean lines, warm lighting, and walls hung with works by Finnish and international artists. It is a place designed for those who want to feel the city rather than simply visit it.

On Friday and Saturday nights, resident DJs set the mood while the bar team puts their craft on full display.

What we serve

Craft cocktails with a view

Our bartenders combine slightly surprising flavours into signature drinks that go well beyond the menu. Alongside them: classics, mocktails, fine wines, and champagne.

Signature Cocktails

Original recipes crafted by the bar team — layered, precise, and designed to complement the atmosphere above the rooftops.

Classic Cocktails

The timeless canon — executed with care using quality spirits, fresh juices, and correct technique.

Mocktails

Zero-alcohol drinks with the same attention to flavour — complex, refreshing, and equally worth the trip up.

Wine & Champagne

A curated selection of bottles chosen to suit the occasion, whether it is a quiet evening or a cause for celebration.

Light Bites

Small savoury plates to share — the ideal companion to an evening of cocktails and conversation.

Cocktail School

Groups of 8–25 can book a guided cocktail-making experience — a shareable, hands-on event above Helsinki. Priced at €64 per person.
