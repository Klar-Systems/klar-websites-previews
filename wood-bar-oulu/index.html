The Bar

Wood Bar is Oulu's only fully wooden bar — a warm, distinctive interior right in the centre of the city, a short walk from the harbour. Draft beers, spirits, cocktails and a bar menu served until 2AM.

Come in after work for a quiet drink, or stay late for Wood Barin Jamit — our live music event series that brings Oulu's best acts to Pakkahuoneenkatu.
