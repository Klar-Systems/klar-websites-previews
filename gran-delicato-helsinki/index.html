Our Story

Helsinki bedrock,
Greek heart

Gran Delicato is not a theme restaurant. It is a living piece of Greece transplanted into the Kamppi neighbourhood of Helsinki. The taverna at Albertinkatu 31 occupies a basement space carved directly into the Helsinki bedrock — shelves built into the living rock, candlelit tables arranged across adjoining rooms, and the quiet warmth of a place that knows exactly what it is.

Owner Konstantinos Barbunakis is almost always present. He selects every wine personally, knows each dish, and will gladly tell you the story behind every glass. All Greek wines, ouzo, and digestifs served at Gran Delicato are his own private imports — available nowhere else in Finland.

The Space

Carved into
Helsinki bedrock

The taverna occupies a basement built into the solid rock beneath Albertinkatu. Stone shelves line the walls. The ceiling is low. The lighting is amber and close. Guests describe it consistently as feeling transported — a small Greek taverna that happens to exist in the heart of Helsinki.

The space accommodates around seven to eight tables arranged across adjoining rooms, making every dinner feel personal. For private events, the entire space becomes yours.

  • Albertinkatu 31, 00180 Helsinki (Kamppi)
  • Tue–Thu 17:00–23:00  |  Fri–Sat 16:00–00:00
  • Basement taverna — carved into Finnish bedrock
  • Intimate — 7–8 tables, up to 30 for private events

Private Dining

The Big Fat
Greek Evening

Gran Delicato hosts private events for up to 30 guests in the full taverna space. Whether a birthday, company dinner, or an occasion that simply calls for something memorable — the stone basement is yours for the evening.

Konstantinos and the team build the menu around your group: mezédes to start, shared plates, wines from the private cellar, and the unhurried pace that makes a Greek dinner what it should be.

  • Up to 30 guests in the full basement taverna
  • Custom menu tailored to your group
  • Wine pairing from exclusive private imports
  • Ouzo and digestif service
  • Konstantinos present to guide the evening
