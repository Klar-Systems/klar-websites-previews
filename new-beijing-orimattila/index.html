About

New Beijing is a Chinese restaurant located in the centre of Orimattila, serving traditional Chinese cuisine since 2019. Known for friendly service and generous portions, we welcome families, workers, and anyone looking for a satisfying meal at honest prices.

Whether you are stopping by for our popular lunch buffet or enjoying an evening dinner, our kitchen prepares each dish with care using traditional recipes.

Lunch Buffet

Our daily lunch buffet features a rotating selection of Chinese dishes, freshly prepared each morning. A Finnish alternative is always included alongside our Chinese specialties.
