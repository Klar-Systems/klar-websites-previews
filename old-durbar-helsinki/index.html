Welcome to Old Durbar

Authentic Nepalese and Indian cuisine in the heart of Helsinki's Kamppi district. With a 4.7 star rating from 295 satisfied customers, Old Durbar brings traditional Himalayan and Indian flavors prepared with care and expertise.

Experience the richness of authentic spice and tradition. Whether you're a connoisseur of Indian cuisine or new to Himalayan flavors, Old Durbar offers a genuine culinary journey.
