About Us

Rovaniemi's Pub for Everyone

Kansan Pubi is the most-loved local pub in Rovaniemi — a warm, unpretentious two-floor venue where students, locals and Arctic tourists all end up on the same bar stool. No attitude, no inflated prices, just a genuinely good night out.

Grab a cold one on the ground floor, rack up the billiard balls, or belt out your favourite song at karaoke. Then head upstairs when the nightclub kicks in at 22:00 on weekends and keep the party going until 04:00.

After an aurora hunt, after a long shift, after absolutely nothing — Kansan Pubi is always the right call.

  • Karaoke nights — join in, no experience required
  • Billiards & table football on the ground floor
  • Big screens for Finnish sports matches
  • Summer terrace — Rovaniemi's best people-watching spot
  • DJ & live music on weekends

Ground Floor — The Pub

Classic pub atmosphere: karaoke stage, billiards, big screens showing Finnish sports, and the coldest cheapest pints in Rovaniemi. Open every evening.

Second Floor — The Nightclub

Rovaniemi's own nightclub upstairs — DJ sets, dancing, and the party energy that keeps the city going until 04:00 on Friday and Saturday nights. Opens 22:00.

What's On

Events & Nights Out

There's always something going on at Kansan Pubi. From karaoke nights to live gigs to DJ sets — check what's happening this week and follow us on Facebook for the full schedule.

Karaoke Night

Regular weekly nights

The mic is yours. Students, tourists, locals — all welcome on stage. Kansan Pubi's karaoke nights are legendary in Rovaniemi. No stage fright allowed (the cheap beer helps).

DJ Weekends

Friday & Saturday from 22:00

The second floor comes alive when the DJ takes over. Dance until 04:00 to the best mixes in Rovaniemi. Entry fee typically 3–15 EUR, cloakroom included.

Live Music

Special event nights

Live bands and artists make the stage their own at Kansan Pubi. Follow us on Facebook for the upcoming gig schedule — these nights fill up fast.

Sports Screenings

Every big match, every season

Big screens, ice cold beer and fellow fans. Finnish football, hockey, and major international fixtures — the best seat in Rovaniemi is at Kansan Pubi.

Happy Hour

22:00 – 23:00 every open evening

Already the cheapest drinks in Rovaniemi — and then there's Happy Hour. Every night we're open, from 22:00 to 23:00, prices drop even further. Don't miss it.

After the Aurora

Open every night until 02:00 or 04:00

Just got back from chasing the Northern Lights? Warm up at Kansan Pubi with a well-earned cold one. Tourists are always welcome — the regulars will share the best aurora stories.

Stay up to date — follow us on Facebook

For the latest event schedule, DJ nights, live gigs and special offers, follow Kansan Pubi on Facebook. Or call us directly on 040 0947693 — we're always happy to tell you what's on tonight.

Happy Hour — Every Night 22:00–23:00

Already the best-priced drinks in town, and during Happy Hour it gets even better. Come early, stay late.

No gimmicks. No overpriced cocktails with long names. Kansan Pubi is about honest drinks at honest prices — which is exactly why it's been Rovaniemi's most talked-about pub for years.

Draft beer from 2.50, shots for 4 EUR, cider and mixed drinks to suit every taste. The bar is fast, the staff are friendly, and you won't spend the whole evening staring at the receipt.

Beers from the tap, a full spirits menu, ciders, and a selection of cocktails and long drinks. Something for every mood and every budget.
