About

A cafe that takes its coffee seriously.

Heritage Cafe is a small independent coffeehouse on Fredrikinkatu in central Helsinki. We pour specialty coffee from rotating roasters — the kind of cup where origin, process and roaster actually matter.

Alongside the coffee, pastries, cakes, sandwiches and quiches, plus vegan-friendly options. Indoor seating, a small outdoor terrace, and local artists’ work on the walls year-round.

Friendly, unhurried, and open from breakfast through evening on weekdays.

Menu

What we serve

A tight menu by design. Coffee we can vouch for, pastries we’d eat ourselves, and light lunches that keep pace with your afternoon.

Specialty Coffee

Espresso, latte, cappuccino and seasonal specialty brews — rotating beans from roasters we know personally.

Pastries & Cakes

A daily selection of pastries and cakes, including gluten-free cookies. Banana bread when the kitchen feels like it.

Sandwiches & Quiches

Light lunch made with good bread — sandwiches and quiches built for an unhurried coffee break.

Hot Chocolate & More

Rich hot chocolate, matcha, and tea — the supporting cast around the coffee programme.

Vegan-friendly options and gluten-free cookies available at the counter.

Private Events

Host your evening at Heritage.

The cafe doubles as a small event venue after hours — private tastings, small birthdays, book launches, art openings with the artist right there at the counter.

Indoor seating plus the terrace when the weather agrees. Customisable packages from coffee-only receptions to full catering.
