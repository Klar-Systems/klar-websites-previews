Hamina's Best-Known Lunch Spot

Itäväylän Bensis has been serving the people of Hamina since 2007, tucked alongside the ST1 service station on Helsingintie. What started as a roadside cafe has grown into the go-to lunch destination for locals, commuters, and anyone passing through on Route 7.

On weekdays the kitchen sends a hot two-course lunch to your table — salad buffet included. Evenings and weekends, the grill takes over with pork cuts, sausages, and salad plates. The famous Päivän Leike — the daily schnitzel — has its own fan following in town.

Finnish-origin meat. A gluten-free lunch option always available. Wolt delivery for when you can't make it in.
