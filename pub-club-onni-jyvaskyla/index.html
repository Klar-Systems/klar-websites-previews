Jyväskylä's Adult Pub

Pub & Club Onni is the most varied pub in central Jyväskylä — a daytime hangout and evening entertainment venue rolled into one. Billiards and board games from opening, karaoke every night, and the city's best pub events calendar from Wednesday through Saturday.

Good drinks at honest prices, a proper terrace and a crowd that knows how to have a good time. On weekends the DJ takes over and Onni becomes the go-to dance venue for Jyväskylä adults.

Group Events

Pub & Club Onni is a popular venue for birthday parties, work nights out and group celebrations. Karaoke, dancing, billiards and a lively crowd make it an easy choice for any occasion.
