About Torin kebab-grilli

Torin kebab-grilli Toijala has been serving Akaa since 1987 from its spot on Toijala square. We grill our own gluten-free Finnish 100% beef kebab daily — generous portions and the same Finnish straight-talk service that's kept regulars coming back for over 35 years.
