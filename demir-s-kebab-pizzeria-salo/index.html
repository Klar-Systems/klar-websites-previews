Why Demir's

Homemade Kebab Meat

Masum Demir makes his kebab meat from scratch, in-house, every day. Sourced from Finnish beef. Gluten-free. There are no shortcuts — the flavour is in the preparation.

That's why Demir's stands out in Salo from the first bite. Not a kebab chain — a craftsman's kitchen.

30 Years of History

Vilhonkatu 15 has been a kebab address in Salo for over 30 years. Masum Demir opened here in January 2025 — bringing his own recipes and a fresh start to a beloved location.

4.3 stars from 371 Google reviews in the opening year. The customers have given their verdict.
