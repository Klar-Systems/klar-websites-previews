Rauhankatu, just off the square

Glory Days has stood on Rauhankatu, just off the Porvoo market square, for years. Bar, café, late-night gathering point — coffee in the afternoon, drinks in the evening, and the crowd that comes with each. Friday and Saturday run late; the rest of the week we keep a slower pace.

Three hundred and forty-seven reviews and a 4.0-star average from a town that doesn't hand them out lightly. Old Porvoo around the corner; the regulars at the bar.

What’s on

The Porvoo crowd knows where to find us. Weekend nights run late with music and conversation; weekdays are for the regulars and the visitors who wander in from the old town.

Late weekends

Friday and Saturday nights run until 04:00 — a slow build, a busy peak, and the kind of evening you remember the next morning.

Old-town stop

Just off the market square, two minutes from Vanha Porvoo. Step in for a coffee, stay for a drink.

Quiet weekdays

Mondays through Thursdays we keep the pace down. Read, work, talk — whatever you came in for.
