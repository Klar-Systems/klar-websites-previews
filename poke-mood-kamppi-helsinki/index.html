About

A bowl for every mood.

Poke Mood Kamppi sits on Eerikinkatu, a short walk from Kamppi shopping centre. Everything is built to order at the counter — pick a signature bowl, or choose your own base, protein, toppings and sauce.

Every bowl comes with mixed salad, sweet corn, edamame, cucumber, tomato, wakame, pineapple, teriyaki sauce and roasted sesame. Add your favourite protein on top.

Vegan? The Inari Tofu & Avocado Bowl is on the menu and on the same counter.
