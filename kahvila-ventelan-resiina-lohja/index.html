About Us

Established in 2013, Kahvila Ventelän Resiina is a charming café with an idyllic railroad atmosphere located near the Lohja train station.

We specialize in freshly baked pastries and filled savory goods prepared daily, paired with excellent coffee. Our cozy space has become a beloved local gathering spot for travelers and regulars alike.
