About

A bar where
culture comes first

Vakiopaine is Jyvaskyla's most beloved cultural bar — 4.5 stars from 392 Google reviewers. Located at Kauppakatu 6 in central Jyvaskyla, it brings together university students, artists, and bohemian regulars under one roof.

The basement hosts theatre performances. The walls carry rotating art exhibitions. The stage welcomes underground and live music acts. And behind the bar: Finnish craft beers including HIISI on tap, alongside Sandels and a solid drink selection.

"Jyvaskyla's most characterful and cosy place. A rich offering of art experiences."

What we offer

  • Live music nights with underground and local bands
  • Basement theatre performances and shows
  • Rotating art exhibitions on the bar walls
  • Craft beer on tap — HIISI Juicy Pale Ale, Sandels and more
  • Welcoming atmosphere for students and regulars alike
  • Central Jyvaskyla location at Kauppakatu 6
