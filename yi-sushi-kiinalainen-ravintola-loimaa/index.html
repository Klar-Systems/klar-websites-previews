About us

Loimaa's Best Asian Table

Since 2018, Yi Sushi has been serving the people of Loimaa a broad selection of Chinese cuisine and fresh sushi in a relaxed, compact setting. Whether you are stopping in for the weekday lunch buffet, sitting down for a family dinner, or ordering takeaway, the kitchen keeps things straightforward, friendly, and full of flavour.

With over 380 Google reviews and a 4.4-star rating, we are proud to be the most-reviewed Asian restaurant in the area — and we are still the same small, welcoming place we have always been.
