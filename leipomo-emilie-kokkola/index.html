About the bakery

A cozy spot in Kokkola.

Bakery & Café Emilie is a neighbourhood bakery where you can pick up fresh pastries to take away or settle in and enjoy them on-site. The café has several table groups and a separate room available for private events and celebrations — for gatherings of up to approximately 40 guests.

Gluten-free options are available daily. Free parking is right outside.
