About Roji

Roji is a modern Japanese sushi restaurant in the heart of Kallio. Known for its quality ingredients, skillful sushi craftsmanship, and daily changing specialty dishes.

Daily specialty dishes at affordable prices — changes every day. A favorite lunch and dinner spot in Kallio.
