Our Selection

Wines

A carefully curated wine list spanning European and New World selections. Our staff know every bottle and are happy to guide you to your perfect glass.

Beers and Craft Drinks

Quality beers and craft options alongside the wine list. Whether you prefer wine or something else, Vox has you covered.

Cheese Boards

Cheese selections curated to pair with your wine of choice. The combination that keeps regulars coming back — guests call it delicious every time.
