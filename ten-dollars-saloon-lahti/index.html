Lahti's Rock Karaoke Bar

Ten Dollars Saloon has been rocking Lahti's Vapaudenkatu since 2001. This is the city's rock-themed karaoke institution — honest prices, a proper saloon atmosphere and one of the best late-night closing times in Lahti.

Whether you're here for karaoke, pool, drinks or just a good night out, the Saloon delivers. The crowd comes back because the Saloon doesn't pretend to be something it isn't.

Karaoke & Happy Hour

Karaoke runs nightly at the Saloon — bring a song, bring your friends, or just come to watch. The stage is open to everyone.

Happy hour makes the early evening the smart time to arrive — drinks at reduced prices before the night crowd rolls in.
