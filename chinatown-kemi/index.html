About Us

A Family Restaurant with Authentic Chinese Roots

ChinaTown has been serving Kemi from Keskuspuistokatu for years. We are a small, family-run restaurant where the kitchen is built around real Chinese cooking — woks, fried rice, noodle dishes, spring rolls — alongside sushi, kebab, pizza and grills. Every meal comes with a salad, and the portions are known across the city for being generous.

  • Authentic Chinese kitchen run by a Chinese family
  • Wide menu: Chinese wok, sushi, kebab, pizza, grill
  • Big portions at moderate prices
  • Friendly, welcoming staff every visit
  • Wheelchair accessible
