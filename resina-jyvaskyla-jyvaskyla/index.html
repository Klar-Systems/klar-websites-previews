The Heart of Jyväskylä Nightlife

Resina is Jyväskylä's legendary karaoke pub — the city-centre spot where the night truly begins. A karaoke stage and dance floor under one roof, with a crowd that knows how to have fun.

Whether you're warming up the week with a quiet Thursday pub quiz or closing out Friday on the dance floor, Resina delivers the city's best karaoke experience — consistent sound, huge song library, and staff that make everyone feel welcome.

  • Karaoke stage with top-class sound system
  • Dance floor and late-night disco from 22:00 on Fri & Sat
  • Pub quiz every Thursday at 20:30
  • Sunday bingo from 21:00
  • 225 seats — room for groups big and small
