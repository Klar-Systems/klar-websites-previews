About
Interior photo coming soon

Seinäjoki's grill since day one.

Jerikon Grilli sits on Maakunnankatu 7 in the heart of Seinäjoki — a no-nonsense grill restaurant that has earned 520 Google reviews and a 4.1-star rating through one simple formula: generous portions, friendly service and late hours when the city needs them most.

The menu is built around Finnish grill staples — burgers, sausage fries, kebab and grilled plates — all made with Finnish beef, pork and lamb. The kitchen is open until midnight every weekday, and until 4 AM on Friday and Saturday nights.

No reservations needed. Walk in, call ahead, or order via Foodora. The busiest nights are Friday and Saturday evenings — if you're coming late, calling ahead is the smart move.
