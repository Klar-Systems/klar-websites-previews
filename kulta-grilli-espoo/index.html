Our story

A real grilli on the E18

Kulta-Grilli is a classic Finnish roadside grilli kiosk sitting right on the E18 motorway between Helsinki and Turku, in Nupuri, Espoo.

For decades, travellers heading west or east have pulled off the E18 for a proper hamburger, a hot meat pie, or a cup of coffee and a pastry. That tradition keeps going — the kind of honest, unpretentious road food that Finland does well.

No reservations, no fuss. Pull in, order, and get back on the road fed and satisfied.
