Järvenpää's Grill Legend

Liisan Grilli has been serving original Finnish hamburgers and classic grill food in Järvenpää since 1990. Over three decades later, it is still run by the same family — and still the first place locals think of when hunger strikes after dark.

The menu is straightforward: homemade burgers, meat pastries, and grilled sausages. No shortcuts, no chain recipes — just the real thing, made the way it has always been done. With 574 Google reviews and a 4.4-star rating, Liisan Grilli has earned its place as the most reviewed grill in Järvenpää.

Open evenings every day — late on weekends until 2 AM. Mon–Wed 17–23 · Thu 17–00 · Fri–Sat 17–02 · Sun 16–22
