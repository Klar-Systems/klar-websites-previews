About Us

Eleven 2 Grilli is Jämsä's premier grill destination. We specialize in flame-grilled burgers, kebabs, and fast food classics that are made fresh every day.

From sausage fries to kebab plates, every item is prepared with quality ingredients and bold flavors. Whether you're hungry for a quick lunch or a satisfying dinner, we deliver authentic grill flavors every time.
