Our Story

Finland's First Shot Bar

Shot Bar Helsinki opened as the first bar in Finland dedicated entirely to shots. We keep it simple: an enormous wall of shots, cold drinks, and bartenders who know what they're talking about. You pick a number, spin the wheel, or trust us — we'll make sure you start the night right.

Located right in the heart of Helsinki on Yliopistonkatu 8, we're an easy walk from Rautatientori and Helsingin yliopisto station. Whether you're kicking off your Friday, wrapping up a work week, or celebrating something special — Shot Bar is the place.
