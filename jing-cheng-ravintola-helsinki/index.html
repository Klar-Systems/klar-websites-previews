About the restaurant

A neighbourhood favourite since day one

Jing Cheng Ravintola sits on Eteläinen Hesperiankatu, a quiet street in Kamppi, just moments from the city centre. Over the years it has built a loyal following drawn back week after week by the same things: large, satisfying portions, fair prices, and a calm room where you can actually have a conversation.

The kitchen serves traditional Chinese — starters, soups, chicken, beef, duck, pork, seafood, and rice and noodle dishes — alongside weekday lunch specials that keep the office crowd coming back. Vegetarian options are always available, and delivery is available through Wolt and Foodora.

Ranked #9 of 182 Chinese restaurants in Helsinki on RestaurantGuru, with a Google rating of 4.4 from 355 reviews — one of the highest-rated neighbourhood Chinese restaurants in the city.
