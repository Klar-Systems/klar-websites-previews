The Whole-Meat Burger

Rose Grilli is famous for its kokoliha (whole-meat) burgers — thick, juicy patties that keep locals coming back. The double kokoliha burger with two 100 g patties has earned the title "best burgers in Ylivieska" from review after review.

Pair it with the salad bar, crispy fries, or go for the full grill experience with sausages, kebab, and starters.
