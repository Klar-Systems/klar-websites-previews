About

A real Helsinki bar.

Harry's Bar Kruna is a small neighbourhood pub on Liisankatu in Kruununhaka — "Kruna" to anyone who lives nearby. Steps from the University of Helsinki, at the kind of corner where regulars know each other and the prices stay reasonable.

"Tiny hole in a wall but has to be the most quaint bar I've ever been in. Honestly feels like a set of a romcom."

Inside: comfortable seating, rock on the radio, a proper drinks selection. Outside on Liisankatu: street seating when the weather agrees. Nothing fancy, nothing polished. Just a genuine Helsinki pub doing what a pub should do.

What we pour

A short, honest drinks list. Typical tab €10–15 per person.

Draught Beer

A rotating draught lineup at Helsinki-friendly prices. Some of the cheapest pints you’ll find in the city centre.

Wine

A small selection of wines by the glass and bottle — nothing over-curated, just what works with the room.

Spirits & Cocktails

Whiskies, gins, and classic cocktails from behind the small bar. Shots for when the evening takes that turn.

Bar Snacks

Simple bar snacks to go with a round. No full kitchen — just enough to keep the evening moving.

Prices and selection can change. Ask at the bar — we’ll sort you out.
