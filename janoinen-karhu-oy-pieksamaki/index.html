What we are known for

Two signatures, one stopover

Everything here comes from the same place it always has — the fryer, the smokehouse, and the kitchen. No shortcuts.

Lampimät Munkit

Warm donuts pulled straight from the fryer — the signature that has drawn Route 9 travellers off the highway since the cafe opened. Order them the moment you arrive.

Lampimänsavulohi

Salmon smoked on-site in the smokehouse at the back of the property. Eat it on the terrace or take a whole piece home — available through the summer season.

Terrace & Smokehouse

Nearly 100 seats on a terrace overlooking Ahvenkoski rapids and Lake Mehtio. Motorhome parking on-site, boat access from both Mehtio and Ahveninen lakes.

What is on the table

From the kitchen & grill

Home-cooked Finnish lunch and cafe fare. Seasonal — the full menu runs May through early October.

Lounasbuffet

Freshly prepared home-cooked lunch buffet, daily during the summer season.

Kalakukko

Local Savonian fish pie — a regional classic you will not find everywhere.

Grillihampurilaiset

Traditional grilled burgers with whole-meat patties, cooked to order.

Kotitekoiset Lihapullat

House-made meatballs — a favourite with families and a staple of the lunch buffet.

Makkaraperunat

Hearty sausage and fries — the classic road-stop plate, done right.

Paulig Juhla Mokka

Finnish cafe coffee served with the donuts and pastries throughout the day.
