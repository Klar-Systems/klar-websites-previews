About Jolene

Pori's Neighbourhood
Karaoke Bar

Jolene is a genuine neighbourhood pub and karaoke bar in the centre of Pori — open from 10 in the morning, every single day. Whether you're a regular or a first-timer, the mic is yours.

3.8 / 5 on Google · Karaoke aamusta iltaan Porin keskustassa

Billiards, board games, a terrace for summer evenings, and a crowd that knows how to have a good time. No attitude, no dress code — just Pori at its most itself.
