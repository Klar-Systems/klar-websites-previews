About

Pan-Asian Flavors in the Heart of Jyväskylä

NÔM Asian Cuisine brings the flavors of Asia to central Jyväskylä. Located in the Agora building on Puistokatu, NÔM offers an all-day buffet featuring sushi, Southeast Asian curries, stir-fries, noodles, and desserts — all in a stylish, contemporary setting.

With generous vegan and vegetarian options, a welcoming atmosphere described as cozy and charming, and prices starting from just 11.90, NÔM has earned a 4.4-star reputation from over 700 satisfied guests.

The Buffet

All-Day Asian Buffet

A generous spread that changes throughout the day. Sushi hand-rolled on site, warm Southeast Asian dishes, fresh salads, and house desserts.

Sushi

A rotating selection of fresh sushi, including dedicated vegan sushi options prepared separately. Maki, nigiri, and specialty rolls.

Warm Dishes

Curries, stir-fried noodles, fried rice, ginger chicken, chili dishes, and tofu options. Southeast Asian flavors with generous portions.

Salads & Sides

Fresh salad table with Asian-inspired dressings. Deep-fried sweet potato, steamed vegetables, and seasonal sides.

Desserts & Drinks

House desserts, ice cream, and caramel selections. Tea and coffee included with every buffet.
