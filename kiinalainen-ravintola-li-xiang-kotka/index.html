About us

Good food, good value.

Li Xiang is Kotka’s go-to for Cantonese cooking. Built on a simple promise — fresh ingredients, generous portions, and prices that make sense — the restaurant has earned a loyal following of regulars who return week after week.

The kitchen follows Cantonese tradition: clean flavours, quality produce, and dishes that speak for themselves. Whether you are here for a relaxed sit-down meal or picking up on the way home, you leave satisfied.
