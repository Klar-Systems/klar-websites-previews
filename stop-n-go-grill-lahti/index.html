The Legend

30 years on Route 4 — and still going strong

Stop'n Go Grill has been standing at the Harju rest area on Route 4 in Lahti for nearly 30 years. It is a proper truck-stop grill that never locks its doors — open at midnight, open at 4 a.m., open on Christmas morning.

Lorry drivers, road workers, night-shift nurses, long-haul travellers — everyone ends up here sooner or later. The portions are generous, the prices honest, and the coffee is always on.

"Look up when you walk in. Coffee cups hanging from the ceiling — collected over decades by regulars. Every cup tells a story."
