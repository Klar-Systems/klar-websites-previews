Our story

A Pub Worth the Drive

Patovahti sits at the edge of the Kellokoski dam, in one of Uusimaa's most atmospheric settings — a 19th-century ironworks district where the river still runs strong.

We open evenings only, from 16:00. Come for a burger that reviewers call the best in Uusimaa, stay for the company, the live music, or a late-night karaoke session on the weekend.

No pretence. No lunch rush. Just good food, honest drinks, and a proper local atmosphere.
