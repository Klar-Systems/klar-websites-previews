Welcome

A family-friendly grill restaurant on Rannikkotie

Sun Steak House is a neighbourhood grill restaurant in Siuntio, serving generous portions of steaks, pizzas, kebabs, and burgers. With a 4.1-star rating across 317 Google reviews, guests keep coming back for the food and the friendly service.

The spacious dining area seats groups of all sizes, and the outdoor terrace offers views of the surrounding meadows -- a perfect spot for a relaxed dinner in the countryside.
