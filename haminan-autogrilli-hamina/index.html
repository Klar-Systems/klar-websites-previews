The Story

Hamina’s legendary drive-in — voted best grill in Southeast Finland

Haminan Autogrilli sits on Rautatienkatu, a short walk from the bus station and the old fortress town centre. Pull up to the window, order without leaving your car — or park and walk straight to the counter. Either way, you eat fast and you eat well.

The makkaraperunat here are famous enough that regulars make the drive from neighbouring cities just for them. With over 571 Google reviews and a 4-star rating, this is not a hidden gem — it is Hamina’s go-to grill.
