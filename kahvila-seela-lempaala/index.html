The story

An old villa. A lakeside terrace. Everything made in-house.

Kahvila Seela opened in 2022 in Rantaniitty, Pirkkala — inside a nearly century-old villa that was originally built as a factory director's summer home. The renovation preserved the building's 1930s character: period furniture, multiple rooms for coffee service, and the kind of quiet you can only find by the water.

Owner Petteri Soini runs the café personally — present every evening on weekdays and throughout the weekends. All food is prepared in the café's own kitchen. In 2024 Soini was named Pirkkala's Entrepreneur of the Year.

In good weather, borrow a SUP board and take the lake with you.

Events & specials

More than just coffee.

Kahvila Seela regularly hosts cultural events in its historic setting — from live piano evenings to the annual Allsång på Seela outdoor singing event. Follow the café on Instagram or Facebook for the latest programme.

Recurring

Live piano evenings

Occasional live piano music during café hours — announced on social media. The historic villa makes for an intimate setting.

Annual

Allsång på Seela

An outdoor community singing event on the café grounds — inspired by the Swedish Allsång på Skansen tradition. Free admission. Held in the Rantaniitty park each summer.
