About Us

Golden Lotus brings the authentic flavors of Asia to Pietarsaari. Our kitchen specializes in Vietnamese, Thai, and Japanese dishes, prepared with the finest ingredients and traditional techniques.

Whether you dine in with us or enjoy our lunch buffet, every dish is crafted to deliver genuine Asian taste and hospitality. Join us for an unforgettable culinary experience.
