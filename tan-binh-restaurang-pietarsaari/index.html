About Us

Vietnam Comes to Pietarsaari

Tan Binh Restaurang brings authentic Vietnamese flavours to Pietarsaari. From fresh Goi Cuon summer rolls and classic Banh Mi to hearty chicken, beef and shrimp rice dishes, every plate is crafted for genuine taste and great value.

Operating since 2012, Tan Binh is one of Pietarsaari's most established Vietnamese kitchens — known for kind, welcoming service and consistently honest food at accessible prices. With 249 Google reviews and a 4.2-star rating, the restaurant has earned its place as a local favourite.
