From the kitchen

Simple, honest summer cafe food — everything baked in-house, coffee brewed fresh, and nature right outside the door.

Munkki & pastries

Warm doughnuts with blueberry jam — the house favourite. Plus house-baked sweet and savoury pastries daily.

Filter coffee

Rich, aromatic filter coffee brewed throughout the day. The perfect pair with anything from the oven.

Savoury & seasonal

Savoury pancakes, seasonal specials, and occasionally smoked salmon from local waters.

In the forest on Porkkalantie

Cafe Porkkala sits along Porkkalantie, just 200 metres from the Porkkala marina and deep in the forest near the northern tip of the Porkkala Peninsula. It’s a stopping point for cyclists, hikers, and day-trippers who come out to breathe.

Open Tuesday to Sunday from late spring to the end of August, and by arrangement for private events in winter. The doughnuts sell out. Come early.
