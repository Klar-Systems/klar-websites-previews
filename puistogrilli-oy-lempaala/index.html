About Puistogrilli

Puistogrilli Oy has been feeding Valkeakoski since 2014. What started as a small grilli kiosk has grown into the city's most-reviewed dining spot — 733 Google reviews and a 4.3-star rating that keeps climbing.

The menu is Finnish grilli at its best: big burgers, honest steak platters, crispy baskets, meat pasties, and all the classics. Generous portions at straightforward prices — the kind of food that families and locals keep coming back for.

Available both for pick-up at the kiosk on Sääksmäentie 18 and for home delivery through Wolt.
