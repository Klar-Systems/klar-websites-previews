About us

Interior photo coming soon

A proper rest stop. No shortcuts.

Stop Cafe Nurmijärvi — known locally as Peltibaari — sits right on Hämeenlinnanväylä (Valtatie 3) at the Karhunkorpi rest area, halfway between Helsinki and Hämeenlinna. Founded in 2021, it quickly became the go-to stop for drivers, commuters and anyone who needs a real break.

Every pastry, roll and bun is baked from scratch on-site, fresh every day. No factory lines, no reheated goods — just honest food made with care.
