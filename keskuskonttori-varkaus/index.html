Our Story

A Living Cultural Landmark in Varkaus

Nestled inside a historic wooden building dating to the 1840s, Keskuskonttori is far more than a restaurant. Across nearly 1,900 square metres and three floors you will find an Italian trattoria, a gallery, a hostel, event spaces — and six resident cats who roam the dining room freely.

The restaurant is run by Luca and Katja, an Italian-Finnish couple who opened their doors with a clear vision: create an honest, living space that brings people together. Luca's Italian heritage shapes the kitchen; Katja's Finnish sensibility shapes the warmth of the welcome.

Everything here is intentional. The furniture is recycled or handmade. The walls carry local art and archival photographs of Varkaus's industrial past. Ideas are always evolving — because Keskuskonttori, above all, wants to make things possible.

Finland's Only Cat Restaurant

Six Resident Cats. One Unforgettable Meal.

Keskuskonttori holds a distinction unlike any other restaurant in Finland: six cats live here and wander freely among the tables. They are not a gimmick — they are part of the atmosphere, as much as the warm lighting and the smell of fresh pizza from the kitchen.

Guests consistently mention the cats as a highlight of their visit. Families with children love it. Solo travellers find unexpected company. And everyone leaves with a story.

"Cats are good therapists and stress relievers. Their presence brings joy and contentment."
— Luca & Katja

Food & Drink

No Printed Menu. Just Good Food.

Pizza — Varkaus's Finest

Authentic Italian pizzas, known well beyond the city limits. Made fresh daily, including gluten-free options. Guests routinely call it the best pizza in Varkaus — and they keep coming back to prove it.

Lunch Buffet

A generous weekday lunch buffet served until 19:00, reflecting whatever is freshest and best that day. After 19:00, the kitchen turns to pizza.

Tiramisu — Grandmother's Recipe

Luca's grandmother's tiramisu recipe, made the old way. A dessert that guests still talk about long after leaving Varkaus.

Pastries & Coffee

Homemade baked goods and good coffee throughout the day. A natural stopping point whether you are here for an hour or an afternoon.

We do not use a printed menu. Food is prepared fresh each day based on availability and what is at its best. The kilometre-zero ("kilometri nolla") philosophy means we do not buy anything new if we can avoid it — everything is sourced, recycled, or made in-house.
