Our story

Where Chinese tradition meets Japanese craftsmanship

Panda Sushi / Kiina Ravintola opened in summer 2020 on Fredrikinkatu in Hamina. The restaurant is owned by Rui Zhang, who brings over 30 years of kitchen experience. The premises house two separate kitchens — one dedicated exclusively to sushi preparation. With more than 300 m² of floor space on street level and 70 seats, the restaurant offers both buffet and a la carte dining. Located in a former bank building, guests can also pre-order sushi platters for pick-up.

Signature concept Two independent kitchens: a dedicated sushi kitchen with its own sushi chef and approximately 30 daily-rotating sushi varieties — party platters available to order from 36 to 60 pieces.
