About

The Chinese buffet Oulu keeps coming back to

With 441 Google reviews and a 4.2-star rating, Merikosken Kiinalainen ravintola has earned its reputation as one of the best Chinese restaurants in Oulu.

Located in the Tuira district at the corner of Merikoskenkatu and Koskitie, the restaurant serves a generous lunch buffet with fresh salad bar, hot wok dishes, spring rolls, sushi, and desserts — all at a price that keeps regulars coming back.

The recently renovated interior is bright and welcoming, with cozy booths and windows on multiple sides. Customers consistently praise the friendly, multilingual staff and the excellent value for money.
