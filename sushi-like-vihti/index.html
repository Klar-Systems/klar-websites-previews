What We Serve

Sushi & Sashimi

Fresh sushi rolls, nigiri and assortment platters made in-house daily. Properly seasoned rice — the detail that keeps guests coming back.

Lunch Buffet

Weekdays 10:30–14:30. Warm Asian dishes, salads, soups, noodles, and a fresh sushi selection — refilled regularly from the kitchen.

Chinese Specialties

Beef, chicken, pork and seafood dishes with rice and noodles. Generous portions, classic Chinese-Finnish flavours.

Vegetarian Options

Falafel, tofu and vegetable dishes alongside the full menu. Clearly marked, always available.

Appetizers

Miso soup, tempura, edamame and spring rolls to start. Pair with any main or as a lighter lunch choice.

Takeaway

Full menu available for takeaway and Wolt delivery. Call ahead or order online — ready when you are.
