The Story

A neighbourhood bar that punches above its postcode.

Since 2010, Jams Bar has been the default meeting point in central Lieto. It is not a dining room in a dinner jacket — it is a rock-pub with a grill out back, a karaoke stage, a sauna to rent and a festival lineup that has brought Uniklubi, Anssi Kela and Kilpi through the door.

The regulars call it "Liedon keskustan ainoa ja hienoin baari" — the only and finest bar in central Lieto. Honest drinks, honest bar food, a night that remembers itself.
