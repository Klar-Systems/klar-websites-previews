About

Your local bar
in Kuopio

Antura pub is Kuopio's most popular karaoke bar — a small, lively, and genuinely welcoming neighbourhood bar at Käsityökatu 25, near Snellman Park. With 378 Google reviews, it has earned its reputation as the city's go-to karaoke spot.

Whether you are a regular or a first-timer, the atmosphere at Antura is always warm and homely. Staff are courteous, drinks are affordable, and the karaoke stage is always ready. Open daily from 11:00 to 02:00.

"A small and popular local bar in Kuopio, known for its lively atmosphere and great karaoke music."

What we offer

  • Kuopio's best karaoke — microphone always ready
  • Cold beers and a full drinks selection
  • Homely neighbourhood atmosphere
  • Open daily until 02:00
