Our Story

A Wine Bar
Built with Passion

Nooran Viinibaari was born from a simple idea: Hameenlinna needed a place where you could unwind with a good glass of wine — without a dress code or a formal occasion.

Owner Noora Vennamo designed and built the bar herself, investing every personal saving into creating a space that feels genuinely welcoming. Today the wine bar shares its home at Kasarmikatu 4 with Juliuksen Olohuone.

Rotating art exhibitions, a street-side terrace in summer, and a carefully curated wine list make every visit a little different.
