The Cellar Story

Below Street Level

Cellar Café & Bar sits below the street level on Jokikatu 55 in old Porvoo. Stone walls, candlelit atmosphere, and the sound of the river nearby — a genuinely unique setting in one of Finland's most historic towns.

In summer, the riverside terrace opens up beside the old bridge — one of the most scenic spots in all of Porvoo for an afternoon drink.

Good Drinks, Good Atmosphere

Known for mulled wine, sangria, quality ciders, and a well-priced drinks selection. Good snacks alongside. The kind of place that regulars guard jealously and tourists discover with delight.

Whether you're a Porvoo local or exploring the old town for the first time — step down and find us.

What We Serve

Wines and Spirits

A well-chosen wine list plus spirits and cocktail options. Mulled wine and sangria are house favourites — guests come back for them every season.

Beers and Ciders

Local and international beers alongside quality ciders. Something for every taste, always at a fair price.

Cocktails and Sangria

House-made sangria and seasonal cocktails. The perfect pairing for a summer evening on the riverside terrace.

Snacks

Good snacks to accompany your drinks. Simple, well-executed, affordable — exactly what a cellar bar should do.
