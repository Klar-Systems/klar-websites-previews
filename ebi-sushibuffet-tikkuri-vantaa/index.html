About Us

Ebi Sushibuffet Tikkuri offers an authentic Japanese dining experience with a diverse selection of fresh sushi prepared daily.

Located in the Tikkuri shopping center, we pride ourselves on high-quality ingredients, diverse sushi options, and exceptional value for our guests seeking an all-you-can-eat sushi experience.
