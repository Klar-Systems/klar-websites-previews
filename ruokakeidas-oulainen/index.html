Oulainen’s go-to grill

Ruokakeidas — also known as Oulaisten Grilli ja Pizza — has been serving the people of Oulainen from Asemakatu 1, right in the city centre. Pizza, kebab, burgers, salads, ice cream — under one roof at prices that make sense.

We have an A-licence, wheelchair-accessible entrance, and a full dining room. Whether you eat in or take away, we’ve got you covered seven days a week.
