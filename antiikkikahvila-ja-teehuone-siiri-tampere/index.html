Our Story

A Living Piece of Finnish History

Antiikkikahvila ja teehuone Siiri opened in December 2016 when owner Leena Soini breathed new life into a magnificently restored 1800s log villa on the shore of Lake Pyhäjärvi — just 20 minutes south of Tampere in Lempäälä.

The café occupies rooms furnished with antiques, where natural light pours over weathered wooden surfaces and flower arrangements. Multiple intimate rooms, a glazed lakeside terrace, and an open-air terrace make every visit feel distinct. You can even arrive by boat — two mooring spots are available at the jetty.

Leena Soini was awarded the 2022 Vuoden Lempo — the municipality's highest recognition — for her role in reviving this beloved historic villa.

Signature

Siiri's Cinnamon Rolls

Soft, generously buttered korvapuusti — the café's most celebrated creation. Guests travel from across the Tampere region just for these. "The best cinnamon roll I have ever had."

Daily Lunch

Soup of the Day

A fresh soup lunch served Wednesday through Friday — classics like salmon soup or vegetable soup, always made from scratch. Allergen information provided. Vegan-friendly options available.

Tea House

Premium Tea Selection

A carefully curated selection of fine teas from a renowned Austrian tea brand — the perfect companion to an afternoon in the antique-furnished rooms overlooking the lake.

Pastries

Homemade Bakes

Cakes, tortes, brownies, chocolate muffins, and spinach rolls — all made in-house daily. Custom celebration cakes available on request.

Vegan & Gluten-Free

Vegan pastries are a permanent fixture on the menu — chocolate brownies, muffins, and Karelian pies. Gluten-free alternatives also available. Everyone is welcome.
