Jyväskylä's Pizza Stop

Pizza Stoppi is Jyväskylä's go-to neighbourhood pizzeria on Sepänkatu. Fast, no-fuss, always fresh — the kind of place that delivers exactly what it promises.

What sets Pizza Stoppi apart: night delivery. When it's late and you need a real pizza, Stoppi is still on. Order through Wolt or Foodora, or call directly.
