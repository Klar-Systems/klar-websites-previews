Our story

A warm corner of the Middle East in Malmi

Bagdad Cafe sits at the heart of Malmi on Kirkonkyläntie 10 — a neighbourhood café-bar that has made itself a genuine local institution. Step inside and the everyday hustle fades: warm colours, the gentle haze of the shisha lounge and the scent of spiced grills do the rest.

The kitchen draws on the full breadth of Middle Eastern cooking — wraps, falafels, shawarma, specialty pizzas and handmade sweets — alongside a daily lunch offer at a price that keeps regulars coming back every week. Come evening, the café shifts into late-night mode, welcoming guests all the way to 01:00 on weekdays and 02:00 on weekends.

Whether you are dropping in for a quick falafel at noon, sharing a shisha with friends in the evening, or lingering over baklava long after the rest of the city has gone to sleep, Bagdad Cafe is ready.

Private events & group visits

Bagdad Cafe is a popular spot for informal gatherings, community events and late-night celebrations. We welcome groups of all sizes and can accommodate private arrangements on request.

  • Birthday & celebration dinners Gather your friends around a table piled with shawarma, wraps and sweets. The kitchen can prepare large-group orders on advance notice.
  • Shisha evenings The shisha lounge is a relaxed setting for an evening with friends — open most evenings, ideal for groups wanting a long, unhurried visit.
  • Community & neighbourhood gatherings Bagdad Cafe has long been a meeting point for the Malmi community. Speak to us about hosting your next neighbourhood event.
  • Late-night group visits Open until 02:00 on Saturdays and Sundays — one of the few places in Malmi welcoming guests deep into the night.

For group bookings, private event enquiries or catering questions, call or message us directly — we will do our best to accommodate you.
