Our Story

A Kitchen With Imperial Roots

Chef Han Pi Dong trained in Beijing for three years under a master who had cooked for the imperial court. He brought that tradition to Imatra in 2007, settling on Imatrankoski with one clear goal: keep the flavours exactly as they are in China.

The name Xiangfu means happiness and peacefulness. That spirit runs through every dish — the slow-simmered broths, the hand-pulled noodles, the vegetables kept vivid and never overcooked.

Nearly two decades later, regulars come two to four times a month and new guests discover the same warm room, the same honest cooking, the same family-run welcome that won Imatra's reader vote with 382 voices.
