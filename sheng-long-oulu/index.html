About

Oulu's trusted Chinese restaurant since day one

With 270 Google reviews and a 4.2-star rating, Sheng Long has built a reputation as one of the best Chinese restaurants in Oulu. Located on Hallituskatu 31 in central Oulu, the restaurant is an easy choice for lunch or dinner.

The kitchen is known for its consistently excellent chicken and beef dishes — regulars who have visited for years say the quality never wavers. Portions are generous, prices are reasonable, and the lunch special at 11.50 EUR including salad, main course, dessert, and a drink is one of the best value meals in central Oulu.

Service is kind and fast. The interior has a warm Chinese atmosphere with comfortable seating. Whether you are coming for a weekday lunch or a casual dinner, Sheng Long delivers.
