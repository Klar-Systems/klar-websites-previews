About

One meal. Done well.

Hanki is the Korean word for "one meal" — and that's the whole idea. A small kitchen on Korkalonkatu run by a Korean team who came to Lapland and decided to cook the food they grew up with — bibimbap, kimchi stew, japchae, fried chicken — using the produce, fish and game they found here. Quiet room. Honest portions. The kind of place locals quietly tell visitors about.
