Our Story

Pori's Irish pub, heart and soul

One For The Road was founded over 30 years ago by an Irish man and his Finnish wife, and it has been welcoming Pori locals ever since. The pub sits at Otavankatu 7 in the heart of the city, and from the moment you walk in, the atmosphere tells you this is no ordinary bar.

The mood is warm, unhurried and genuinely welcoming — a place where strangers become regulars and regulars feel at home. Multiple TVs, a projector, board games, and Pori's only pool table round out the experience.

"Like a living room next door" — that's how the pub describes itself, and the 341 Google reviews back it up with a 4.4 rating.
What's On

Live, loud, and full of craic

From live music and pub quizzes to festive private gatherings, there is always something happening at One For The Road. Here is what makes this pub Pori's most full-spirited local.

Live Music

Random live performances are a regular feature at One For The Road. From acoustic sessions to full bands, the stage comes alive at least once a month — often more.

Pub Quiz — Pubivisa

Test your knowledge at our regular pubivisa evenings. Teams form, pints are poured, and the competition is fierce but friendly. Follow our Facebook page for upcoming dates.

Pool Table

One For The Road is the only pub in Pori with a pool table. Rack 'em up and challenge your mates — the table is always free to play.

Board Games

A selection of board games is always on hand for a relaxed evening. Whether you are a couple or a group, pull up a chair and make an evening of it.

Private Events & Pikkujoulut

Looking to host your end-of-year party or a private group evening? One For The Road is a natural setting for pikkujoulut and after-work gatherings. Call us to arrange.

Sports on the Big Screen

Multiple TVs and a projector mean you never miss the big match. The bar fills up fast on game nights — arrive early and secure your spot.

Drinks & Food

Proper pints and pub fare

One For The Road takes its draught seriously. You will find the classics that belong in any self-respecting Irish pub, alongside craft choices for those who like to explore.

  • Guinness Draught The classic, poured right
  • Murphy's Cork's own Irish stout
  • Staropramen Premium Czech lager on tap
  • Fog City CoolHead Brew craft beer
  • Irish Coffee A pub staple, done properly

Full drinks selection available at the bar. Ask our staff for what's pouring fresh tonight.
