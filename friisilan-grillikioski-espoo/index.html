Espoo's Classic Grill

Friisilän Grillikioski has been serving Espoo since 1985. Over 40 years of fresh burgers made to order — no frozen patties, no shortcuts. Just quality ingredients and honest grill food.

From a classic cheese burger at €7 to the legendary Mega Cajun at 1.6kg and €36 — there's something for every appetite at Friisiläntie 2. Outdoor seating available.

Open weekdays from 8AM, Saturdays from 11AM. Wolt delivery also available.
