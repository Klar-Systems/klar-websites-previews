The Venue

Kantakrouvi is Oulu's home for rock and metal music lovers. Multiple floors give you room to breathe between sets, and free pool tables keep the evening going when the stage is between acts.

Local beers, quality ciders, a full bar — all at prices that don't punish you for staying out late. Both indoor and outdoor seating available.
