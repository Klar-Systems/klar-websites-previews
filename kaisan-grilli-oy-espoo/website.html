Our Story

A Classic Stop on Turunväylä

Kaisan Grilli Oy has been serving hot food to travellers and locals along the Turunväylä motorway in Espoo for years. We open at 5 AM so that truck drivers, tradespeople, and early commuters always have a proper hot meal waiting.

No frills, no fuss — just generous portions, fair prices, and the kind of food that actually fills you up. Burgers grilled to order, sausages done right, and a daily lunch that keeps regulars coming back.

Give us a call and we will have your order ready when you pull in.
