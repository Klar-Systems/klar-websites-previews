The Building Has a Story

Pub Virasto occupies what used to be the City of Lahti's technical office — 350 square metres spread across two floors, with high ceilings, original architectural details, and Victorian furnishings that were kept deliberately intact.

The name says it all: this was a government bureau once. Now it's the most characterful pub in Lahti, with full A-licence, a proper beer selection, a sunny street-level terrace, darts boards, a live music stage, and a weekly pub quiz that draws regulars every Wednesday.

Central location, no pretension, exactly the kind of place you can drop into after work or make a full evening of it.
