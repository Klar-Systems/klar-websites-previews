About Us

Teerijärvi's
Favourite Grill

Terjärv Grill Ab has been a beloved part of Teerijärvi village life since 2018. Founded by Jenna Gistö, the grill has built a loyal following across Ostrobothnia for its legendary kebab and warm welcome.
