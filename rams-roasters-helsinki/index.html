Our story

A real artisan bakery. Dope coffee beans.

RAMS Roasters is a small neighbourhood café in Ullanlinna run by Cosmin and Cristina Tatosian. Everything on the counter — the cinnamon buns, the quiches, the cakes — is baked in-house each morning.

On the coffee side, the machine is loaded with beans from carefully chosen European specialty roasters that rotate regularly: The Barn, KAWA, Friedhats, Frukt, and more. Each batch brings something new.

The café sits next to Kaivopuisto park with a terrace out front. Locals come back. Visitors come back. Laptop-free by choice — because some places should stay places.
