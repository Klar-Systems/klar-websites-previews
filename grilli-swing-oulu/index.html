Real Bosnian Flavours in Oulu

Grilli Swing has been bringing authentic Bosnian grill cooking to Oulu's Tuira neighbourhood for years. In a city full of ordinary grill kiosks, this place stands apart: everything is made from scratch, right here. The steaks, the sausages, the cevapcici — and most importantly, the homemade pita bread, baked fresh on the premises every day.

The owner brings genuine passion and warmth to every order. With 693 reviews and a 4.7-star rating on Google, Grilli Swing has quietly become one of the most loved spots in Oulu. Bosnian soul in a Finnish grilli shell.
