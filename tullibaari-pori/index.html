Pori's Night Bar

Tullibaari has been a Pori institution since 2008. Two floors, a large year-round terrace and more entertainment than you'll find anywhere else in the city — karaoke, billiards, table football, pinball and poker all under one roof.

We're open late (Fri–Sat until 5 AM) and built for a good night out. Drinks range from classic beers to craft selections and cocktails at prices that let you stay as long as you like.

Private Sauna Cabin

Tullibaari's private sauna cabin is one of Pori's most popular venues for group celebrations, birthday parties, company events and team nights out.

The cabin accommodates up to 20 people and includes a spacious lounge with TV and sound system, kitchen facilities, and the option to bring your own food and drinks.
