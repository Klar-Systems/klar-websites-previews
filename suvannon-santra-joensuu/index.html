What's On Find Us

A Bar with History

Suvannon Santra sits in an 1850s wooden building in Joensuu's peaceful wooden district — a venue with genuine character that no amount of modern fit-out can manufacture.

Days here are for billiards, good company and affordable drinks. Evenings are for karaoke — nightly, enthusiastic, and welcoming to everyone from debut singers to old hands.

Why Joensuu Comes Here

Nightly Karaoke

Karaoke every single day — from opening to close. A wide song selection and a crowd that lives for it.

Billiards Room

Pool tables available throughout the day. A good excuse to arrive early and stay late.

200-Seat Terrace

A huge summer terrace in the wooden district — the best outdoor seat in central Joensuu.

Historic Building

An 1850s wooden property in the peaceful puutalokortteli — genuine atmosphere you can feel.

Daytime Bar

Affordable drinks and discounts on tap throughout the day. Come when you want, stay as long as you like.

Private Events

Book the venue for group celebrations, birthday parties or company events. Get in touch for details.

Events & Evenings

Karaoke runs nightly from opening until close. Weekly specials and seasonal events appear throughout the year — follow us on Facebook for the latest schedule.
