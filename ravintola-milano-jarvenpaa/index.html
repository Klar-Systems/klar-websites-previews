Our Story

A Taste of Italy,
Right in Järvenpää

Ravintola Milano has been serving Järvenpää since 1998 — over 26 years of classic Italian cooking in a warm, intimate setting just steps from the railway station. We are a small, family-run trattoria where pizza, pasta, and steaks are prepared with care and served with genuine hospitality.

What sets us apart is personal connection. Our owner visits every table — to say hello, check that everything is to your liking, and make your evening a little more memorable. It is an old Italian tradition, and it is one we are proud to keep alive in Järvenpää.
