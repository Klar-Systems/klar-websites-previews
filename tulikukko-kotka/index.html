A Place to Linger by the Water

Tulikukko is Kotka's sunny waterfront pavilion — a café-restaurant and archipelago terminal at the Sapokka marina. Whether you're stopping in after a boat trip, taking a lunch break, or simply chasing the best view in Kotka, this is your spot.

In summer, the sun terrace wraps you in warm sea air. In winter, a warm fireplace does the same from inside. Year-round, the kitchen turns out fresh homemade pastries, lunch plates and a wide selection of specialty teas and coffees that we think you won't find anywhere else in the region.
