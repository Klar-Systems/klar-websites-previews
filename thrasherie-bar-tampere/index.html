Who we are

Tampere's home
of heavy

Thrasherie Bar is Tampere's dedicated metal and rock venue — and has been since it opened its doors at Hameenkatu 30. From rock to black metal, the growing music selection is curated by staff who live and breathe the music.

The bar opens daily at 14:00 and stays open until 02:00 on weekdays, 03:00 on Fridays and Saturdays. The legendary Rassisshot has become a Tampere institution. Song requests are always welcome — just ask the staff.

Located at the corner of Hameenkatu and Hameenpuisto in the heart of Tampere. Credit cards accepted. No attitude required — metal really is for everybody.
