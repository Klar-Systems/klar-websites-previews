Our Story

The Mangal Difference

Itiksen Mangal is a Turkish charcoal-grill restaurant on Hansasilta bridge in Itäkeskus, east Helsinki. We specialize in mangal — the traditional method of grilling over real charcoal, which gives meat a smoky depth no gas grill can replicate.

Every plate arrives with charcoal-grilled meat, sumac onions, grilled tomato and chili, fresh bread, and yogurt sauce. Alongside our grilled dishes, we serve pide, lahmacun, meze, falafel, and homemade soup.

"The soul of the dish is undeniably the meat — fresh, tender and above all well-cooked and seasoned." — Helsingin Ravintolat
