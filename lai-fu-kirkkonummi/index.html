About Lai Fu

Kiinalainen Ravintola Lai Fu is a family-owned Asian restaurant on Kirkkotallintie in Kirkkonummi centre. We serve a daily lunch buffet with sushi and Chinese-Thai dishes for €8.90, plus a full à la carte menu in the evenings — curries, wok dishes, fresh sushi and poke bowls. Same recipes, same warm welcome, every day.
