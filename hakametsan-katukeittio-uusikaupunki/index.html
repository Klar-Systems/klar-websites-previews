Our Story

A place where body and soul are nourished

Hakametsän Katukeittiö — known locally as Hakitsun Grilli — is a fixture in Uusikaupunki's Hakametsä neighborhood. Founded by Tarja Elamo-Lindberg, the grill has been welcoming locals with filling plates and a genuine smile.

We serve traditional Finnish grill classics: kebab plates, the legendary Mersu annos, house specials, and sausage dishes. Large portions, fair prices, and no-nonsense good food.

Over 250 Google reviews and a loyal local crowd tell the story better than we ever could.
