Kantvik's seaside terrace

Café Purje sits on the harbour at Kantvik in Kirkkonummi — a covered terrace right on the water, served by the team behind Huikeet. Salty and sweet treats, hot and cold drinks, and a bar licence for the late-summer evenings. Open through the warm season; quiet and protected when the wind picks up.

Three hundred and forty-eight reviews and a 4.3-star average from a crowd that knows its terraces. Visitors come for the view; they come back for the calm.

What’s on

We host occasional acoustic evenings and harbour-themed gatherings through summer. The bigger draw is just being here — sea view, heaters under the canopy, and a quiet pace that pulls people in for the whole afternoon.

Boater's welcome

The harbour brings new visitors every weekend. Tie up, walk over, and let the terrace do the rest.

Sunset evenings

Late-summer Fridays and Saturdays — the best seats face the water. Get there before sundown.

Acoustic sets

Occasional live music on the terrace through the warm months. Watch our Facebook page for upcoming dates.
