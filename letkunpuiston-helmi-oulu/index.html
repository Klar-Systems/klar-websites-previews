Who we are

The Gem of Oulu's City Centre

Letkunpuiston Helmi is the kind of pub you come to once and keep coming back to. Tucked on Uusikatu 23 right in the heart of Oulu, we have been the city's go-to spot for affordable drinks, great company and a weekly programme that gives you a reason to step out every night of the week.

Spread across two floors — a street-level pub with a large terrace and a downstairs karaoke hall with a dance floor — there is always something happening here. Whether it is a lazy Sunday afternoon beer or a late Friday karaoke session, the door is open and the staff is genuinely glad to see you.

Low threshold, high spirits. Everyone welcome.

Events & Entertainment

Weekly Programme

Something on every night. Check what's coming up and plan your week around Helmi.

Monday – Sunday
Karaoke Night
Daily karaoke in the downstairs hall. Grab the mic, pick your song and let Helmi's legendary underground stage do the rest. Beginners, pros and everyone in between.
Weekly
Bingo
Old-school bingo with a pub twist. Great prizes, plenty of laughs and the best bingo crowd in Oulu. Bring your lucky numbers.
Weekly
Trivia Night
Think you know your stuff? Test your knowledge at Helmi's popular pub quiz. Form a team, order a round and prove it. All topics, all welcome.
Seasonal / Terrace
Jam Sessions
Spontaneous live music on the terrace when the weather plays ball. Local musicians, cold beers and Oulu summer nights. Follow us on TikTok for the latest.
Sunday Special
Cheap Beer Sunday
Beer from €2–2.50 every Sunday. Among the best prices in all of Oulu. The place fills up fast — come early and secure your table.

The Venue

Two Floors — Double the Fun

Ground Floor
Pub & Terrace
The street-level pub floor is all cosy corners and good conversation. Step outside onto our large terrace — one of Oulu centre's best — for a beer in the fresh air. Home to jam sessions and summer evenings you will not forget.
Lower Floor
Karaoke Hall & Dance Floor
Head downstairs and the atmosphere shifts up a gear. The karaoke hall has a proper stage, a dance floor and enough energy to carry a Tuesday all the way to the early hours. This is where the night really happens.
