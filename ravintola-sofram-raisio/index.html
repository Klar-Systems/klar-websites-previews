A local favourite

Don't let the modest exterior fool you. Step inside Sofram and you'll find a clean, spacious dining room with an open kitchen — one that customers consistently single out for its exceptional cleanliness.

With over 366 Google reviews and a steady 4.0 / 5 rating, Sofram has earned its place as Raisio's go-to neighbourhood restaurant for families, lunch crowds, and anyone who wants a proper, filling meal without overpaying.

Free parking nearby makes it easy to visit for a quick lunch or a relaxed dinner.
