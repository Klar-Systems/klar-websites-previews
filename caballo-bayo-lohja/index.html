About Us

Caballo Bayo has been serving Tex-Mex favorites in central Lohja since 1995. Located on the second floor of Kauppakatu 8, our kitchen prepares fajitas, burritos, chili con carne, and grilled dishes with care and generous portions.

In the summer months, enjoy your meal on our rooftop terrace overlooking the city. Whether it is a weeknight dinner or a weekend gathering, Caballo Bayo is the place for relaxed dining with friends and family.
