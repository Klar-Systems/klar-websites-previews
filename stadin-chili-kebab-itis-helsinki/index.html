About Us

Stadin Chili Kebab Itis brings bold flavors and quality ingredients to the heart of Helsinki. With a focus on authentic kebabs and wood-fired pizza, we craft each order with care and pride.

Whether you're grabbing a quick lunch or a satisfying dinner, our menu offers something for everyone. Open daily for dine-in and takeaway, with fast delivery available.
