About us

A Kempele institution since the 1980s

Ramin Grilli has been serving delicious grilled food in Kempele's Sarkkiranta neighbourhood for nearly 40 years. Traditional Finnish grill classics — made fresh, served fast, always affordable.

Order at the counter inside or pull up to the drive-through window. Gluten-free and vegan options are always available.
