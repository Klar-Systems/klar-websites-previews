The place

An old home behind the cathedral.

Vanhan Porvoon Glassikko sits at Kirkkotori 7, a few steps from Porvoo Cathedral. The café is run by chef Samuli Wirgentius out of the family's former home — an 18th-century building with the patina and quiet of its age.

Weekdays are for breakfast, coffee, and a Scandinavian lunch buffet. Weekends are for brunch, served to the table in two sittings. Friday and Saturday evenings, the kitchen turns to pizzas and burgers. And all year round, the adjoining Vanhan Porvoon Jäätelötehdas keeps the ice-cream counter stocked.
