About Us

Big Portions, Great Value — That's Par Piz

Ravintola Par Piz is Parainen's go-to spot for honest, satisfying pizza and kebab. Located at Keskuskäytävä 1 in the town centre, we have built a loyal following of locals who come back for our generous portions and good prices.

From classic pizzas to kebab dishes, wings, falafel, and chicken döner — our menu has something for everyone. Whether you are eating in, ordering delivery on Wolt, or grabbing a take-away, we make sure every plate leaves the kitchen the way it should: full and flavourful.
