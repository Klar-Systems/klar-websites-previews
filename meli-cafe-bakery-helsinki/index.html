Baked Every Night.
Ready Every Morning.

Meli Café & Bakery was opened by Ermin Softic — a Bosnian who moved to Finland 24 years ago — and his wife, in the heart of Kallio on Helsinginkatu. The space was gutted and rebuilt from a former pizza place. The oven fits around a hundred loaves at a time.

Ermin bakes through the night so that when the doors open, everything on the counter is fresh from that morning. No batch baked yesterday. No frozen dough reheated. The rule of the house: no freezer, no microwave.

The result is what draws the queues on Sunday mornings: lepina, kifla and buhtla alongside stuffed baguettes, croissants, omelettes, salads and freshly squeezed juices. Breakfast all day, because as Ermin puts it, there's no good reason to stop serving it at ten.
