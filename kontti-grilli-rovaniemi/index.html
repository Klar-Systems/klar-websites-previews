About

The grill in
the container

Kontti-Grilli is one of Kerava's most distinctive grill stops — 4.0 stars from 394 Google reviewers. The entire restaurant is built inside a large two-storey shipping container at the Leppäkorven rest area on the E75 highway between Helsinki and Lahti.

Whether you are a road-tripper, trucker, or local, Kontti-Grilli serves honest Finnish grill food from early morning. Burgers, premium burgers, grill sausages, hot dogs, large grill dishes, and multiple types of fish pies — all made to order.

"Traditional Finnish grill food. Tasty and unhealthy as it is supposed to be."

What we serve

  • Burgers and premium burgers
  • Grill sausages and hot dogs
  • Large grill dishes
  • Multiple types of fish pies (lihapiirakat)
  • Vegetarian option available (Vegekonttikin)
  • Open from 06:00 on weekdays — ideal for early road stops
