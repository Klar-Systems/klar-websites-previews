A Real Grilli

Lehtimäen Grilli has been serving the Porvoo area with honest, no-frills Finnish grilli food. Located on Hamarintie, it is the kind of place where the food does the talking.

From a properly loaded hamburger to crispy makkaraperunat and golden fries, every item is made to order. Simple, satisfying, and done right.

Call ahead and your order will be ready for pickup — fast and straightforward.
