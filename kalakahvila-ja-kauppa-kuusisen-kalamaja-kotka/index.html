Our story

Off the beaten track, on purpose.

Kuusisen Kalamaja sits where Kotka's fishermen have always unloaded — a small alder-wood shed on the quay, a short walk from the Maretarium. Inside: a handful of tables, a simple counter, and whatever came in fresh that morning.

We are not a big restaurant. We are a fish cafe and a fish shop, open while the daylight lasts, closed when the week ends. Come for a bowl of salmon soup, stay for coffee, and pick up a piece of smoked fish on your way out.

"One of the most authentic dining experiences in Finland. Very simple but very fresh — soup and sandwich." — Wanderlog visitor review

Kalakauppa — the fish shop

Smoked in the morning, sold by the afternoon.

The shop counter is open the same hours as the cafe. We carry what is freshest that week — salmon, whitefish, perch — smoked and ready, or chilled to take home.

If you want something specific for a weekend dinner, phone ahead and we will set it aside.

  • Hot-smoked salmon
  • Cold-smoked salmon
  • Smoked whitefish (siika)
  • Rye bread for sandwiches
  • Preserves and sides
  • Phone orders welcome

Group visits and fish evenings

Bringing a boatload? Tell us first.

We are a small shed. Ten of you at once is a lot of us. If you are planning a club outing, a bus tour on its way to the Maretarium, or a private fish evening outside our normal hours, send us a note and we will work it out.

For smoked-fish orders over a kilo, a day's notice lets us smoke it the morning you collect it.
