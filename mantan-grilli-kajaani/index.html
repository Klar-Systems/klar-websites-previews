About us

A Kajaani Institution

Mantan Grilli-Kioski is a proper Finnish roadside grill kiosk in the Sudenpolku neighbourhood of Kajaani. No frills, no pretence — just large, satisfying portions of classic Finnish grilli food at honest prices.

From the legendary Mega Manta to crispy Kebab Ranskalaiset, every order is made fresh and handed over fast. This is the kind of place Kajaani locals have been coming back to for years.

Order by phone and pick up hot from the kiosk.
