Oulu's Favourite Beer Tavern

Heinäpään Oluttupa has been a cornerstone of Oulu nightlife since 1988. Tucked along Isokatu, this classic Finnish pub offers a warm, unpretentious atmosphere where locals have gathered for decades.

Whether you're stopping in after work for a cold pint or settling in for a long evening with friends, the Oluttupa's traditional tavern spirit makes everyone feel at home.

Over 320 Google reviews and a loyal local following prove that some things only get better with time.
