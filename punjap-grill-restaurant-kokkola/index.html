Made Fresh, Every Time

Punjap Grill & Restaurant brings authentic North Indian cooking to the heart of Kokkola. Every dish is prepared fresh to order — from tender chicken curries and slow-cooked lamb to crispy naan baked in-house.

The tiny kiosk on Torikatu may be small, but what comes out of the kitchen is anything but ordinary. Regulars know to give it 30–45 minutes — because the wait is absolutely worth it.

With over 324 Google reviews and a 4.3 rating, Punjap is Kokkola's open secret for the best Indian food in the region.
