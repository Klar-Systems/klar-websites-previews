The Pub

A Local in the Heart of Vaasa

Littlepub sits right on Vaasa's market square — one of the best spots in the city to watch life go by, whether you're inside with warm wooden walls around you or out on the terrace on a summer evening.

Founded in 2015 by Janne Knuutti and Aleksi Laine, the pub has grown into one of Vaasa's most-reviewed spots: 886 Google reviews and over 12,700 Untappd check-ins from regulars and visitors alike.

The bar staff know their beers and are happy to guide you through the selection. Board games are on the tables. There's a TV for sport. The terrace fills up fast on sunny days — for good reason.

  • Summer terrace on the market square
  • Board games and card games available
  • TV screen for live sport events
  • Live music on occasion
  • Pet-friendly and smoke-friendly outdoor area
