Our Story

Huittinen's kebab & pizza since 2013

Huittisten Eka Kebab-Pizzeria has been serving the people of Huittinen since 2013 — a go-to spot for anyone who wants proper kebab, pizza and salads without waiting around.

Known for generous portions and a salad bar that customers drive out of their way for, EKA has built a loyal following across the city. Over 500 Google reviews and a 4.2 star rating speak for themselves.

Dine in, take away or order by phone — we're open for lunch and dinner, six days a week plus Sundays.
