About Us

Two Floors.
One Night Out.

Bar Taso is Hämeenlinna's city-centre music and karaoke bar — karaoke downstairs, dance floor upstairs. Open Wednesday to Sunday from 22:00.

Whether you're hitting the karaoke mic or dancing until early morning, Bar Taso is the go-to late-night venue in Hämeenlinna's centre.

  • Karaoke stage — ground floor
  • Dance floor — upper level
  • Live music programme
  • Super Sundays — beer & cider deals
  • Open until 04:30 Wed–Sun
