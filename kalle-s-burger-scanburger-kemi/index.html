The story

Not just any burger.
The Kemi Burger.

Kalle's Burger is Kemi's home-grown burger joint — built on a single question: why can't fast food actually be good?

Every patty is handmade from locally sourced Finnish beef. The signature Kemi Burger comes in five variations, each built with Meri-Lapland ingredients, parmesan mayo, and a twist that no chain can copy. The house uses ground meat from Rönka, a local butcher — because the quality shows.

Open six days a week, late on weekends, with over 30 burger variations, Scanburger classics, and enough dip options to keep things interesting.
