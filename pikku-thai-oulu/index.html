Since 2001

A quarter century of Thai flavours in Oulu.

Pikku Thai has been on Asemakatu since February 2001 — over 25 years of serving authentic Thai and Vietnamese cuisine in central Oulu. The menu has grown to over 70 dishes, making it the city's most extensive Thai offering.

"the ingredients were authentic with fresh spices - a real flavour bomb!"— Eat.fi reviewer

Every dish is dairy-free. Protein is sourced from Finland-raised beef, pork, and chicken, while shrimp and squid come from Asian suppliers. The kitchen balances tradition with care for dietary needs.
