About us
Welcome to Bamboo Palace

Bamboo Palace is Mantsala's only Chinese restaurant, serving the community since 2010. Located on Keskuskatu in the center of town, we bring authentic Chinese flavors, fresh sushi, and Thai-inspired dishes to your table.

Our lunch buffet is one of the most popular in Mantsala, featuring a rotating selection of Chinese hot dishes, sushi, Thai specialties, soup, salad, and desserts. In the evening, enjoy our full a la carte menu with table service.

We hold a full A-licence and offer a selection of beverages to complement your meal. Vegetarian and vegan options are always available.
