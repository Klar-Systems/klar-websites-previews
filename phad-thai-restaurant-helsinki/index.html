About Us

Phad Thai Restaurant brings authentic Thai flavors to the heart of Helsinki. Our kitchen specializes in traditional Thai dishes prepared with fresh ingredients and time-honored recipes.

Located in Taka-Töölö, we're committed to delivering an authentic taste of Thailand with warm hospitality and a welcoming atmosphere for all our guests.
