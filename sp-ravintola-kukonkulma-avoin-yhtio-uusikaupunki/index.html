About us

A Laitila favourite since day one

Ravintola Kukonkulma is a lunch and evening restaurant right in the centre of Laitila. We serve a daily lunch buffet on weekdays and offer kebab, stone-oven pizzas and burgers throughout the day. The bar and karaoke make us the area's go-to spot for an evening out. Catering available for private events.

By day a busy lunch restaurant, by evening a bar with karaoke and late opening hours — Kukonkulma is the social hub of the Vakka-Suomi region. The lunch buffet runs weekdays from 10:30 to 14:00.

"Kukonkulma serves the broader Vakka-Suomi region from its Laitila base. Whether you are in for a quick weekday lunch or a relaxed Friday evening with friends, the door is open."
