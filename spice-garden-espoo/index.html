Our Story

A neighbourhood favourite
since day one.

Spice Garden is a small, welcoming Indian restaurant tucked into the Lintuvaara neighbourhood of Espoo. No-frills, no pretence — just authentic North Indian cooking with generous portions and honest prices. What the interior may lack in grandeur, the kitchen more than makes up for on the plate.

The loyal following says it best: regulars travel all the way from Helsinki specifically for the food. Tandoori dishes cooked without shortcuts, curries built from real spices, and spice levels adjusted to your exact preference — from mild to fiercely hot.

Authentic

North Indian recipes using quality ingredients — tandoori and curries the way they should be.

Generous

Portions that consistently surprise guests. Value for money is not an afterthought.

Your heat

Spice level adjusted to order — mild, medium, hot, or very hot. Just ask.

Friendly

A neighbourhood place that treats regulars like regulars. Warm, unpretentious service.
