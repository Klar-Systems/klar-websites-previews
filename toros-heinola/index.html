Our Story

Serving Heinola Since 2006

Toros has been a fixture on Kauppakatu for nearly two decades. What started as a neighbourhood pizza and kebab spot has grown into one of Heinola's most beloved sit-down restaurants — trusted by locals for its hearty portions, reasonable prices, and relaxed atmosphere.

Don't judge us by the facade. Step inside and you'll find a spacious, welcoming dining room with a menu that goes well beyond the basics: hand-stretched pizzas, spiced kebab platters, grilled pihvi steaks, and crowd-favourite chicken dishes — plus weekday lunch specials.
