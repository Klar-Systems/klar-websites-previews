Where the Torne River meets the sea.

The Kukkolankoski is the last free-flowing rapids of the Torne before the Gulf of Bothnia. Salmon, whitefish and centuries-old fishing tradition all pass through here.

"Kukkolan Koski-Catering represents the best salmon soup on Route 4."

The main building is a red-painted wooden restaurant perched on the riverbank, with a full A-licence, a large riverside hall and a terrace right over the rapids.

Next to it: a rental sauna, a grill cottage on the shore, and a motorhome park that stays open through the long northern winter — the rapids never freeze.

Friday and Saturday nights are karaoke nights. Summer brings daily smoked whitefish and Arctic char straight off the fire.

What's on the fire.

Traditional Finnish-Lappish cooking. The salmon soup is what brought most of you here.

The kitchen keeps it honest: salmon soup, reindeer, smoked fish and the grill. Nothing from a freezer truck, and nothing the river couldn't have delivered.
