The Pub

Tuisku Pub has been the heartbeat of Tuira since 1992. Whether you're in for a cold beer after work, a karaoke night with friends, or catching the match on the big screen — this is your place.

Free pool tables, a full bar, and the kind of welcoming atmosphere that keeps regulars coming back year after year. You'll find us on Valtatie 57, right in the centre of Tuira.
