Our Story

Kotka's Best Irish Pub

Pub Jaakko V has been a fixture of Kotka's social life since 2004. Tucked away on Kirkkokatu in the heart of the city, it has earned a reputation as the most welcoming pub in town — an A-licensed establishment where locals and visitors alike feel immediately at home.

The interior carries the warm charm of a proper Irish pub: wooden fittings, comfortable seating, and a relaxed atmosphere that invites you to stay a little longer. Whether you are watching the match on the big screen, joining a Thursday quiz night, or simply enjoying a well-priced drink with friends — this is Kotka's pub.

In summer, outdoor seating opens up along the street, making it a favourite spot from noon to the early hours.
