Our Story

A Grilli Legend Since 1973

Est. 1973

Järvigrilli has fed the people of Oulu for over fifty years. Tucked beside the lake on Järvitie, it started as a roadside kiosk and grew into one of the most beloved fast-food stops in Northern Finland.

The formula has never changed: real ingredients, a generous hand, and prices that make sense. Whether you pull up after work or roll in past midnight on a Friday, the grill is on and the portions are serious.

This is Finnish grilli culture at its most authentic — no shortcuts, no pretension, just food that does the job.
