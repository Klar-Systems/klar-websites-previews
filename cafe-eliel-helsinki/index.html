Why Cafe Eliel

More than a café — a place to stay a while

Art Nouveau Setting

Stone tables, oak panelling, and leather details inside a Museoviraston-protected space with original Saarinen-era tall windows.

Coffee by Löfberg

Three filtered coffees, espresso-based drinks, and Cold Brew — all roasted by Löfberg for a responsible and full-bodied cup.

Helsinki Cocktails

Bar menu with cocktails and drinks emphasising local Helsinki producers — from morning coffee to after-work drinks.

Menu

From morning to evening

Café & Breakfast

  • Filtered coffee — three varieties by Löfberg
  • Espresso-based drinks
  • Cold Brew ice coffee
  • Pastries and cakes — vegan options available
  • Voileipäkakku — Finnish sandwich cake
  • Gluten-free options on request

Lunch & Bar

  • Salad buffet during lunch hours
  • Two warm daily options
  • Eromangan lihapiiras — traditional Helsinki meat pie
  • Sandwiches
  • Cocktails and local Helsinki beverages
  • Dinner dishes in the evening
