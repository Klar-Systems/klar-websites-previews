About Palski

Kotka's Favourite
Summer Gathering Place

Est. on the Rocks of Palotorninvuori

Palski is Kotka's beloved outdoor bar, perched on the rocky heights of Palotorninvuori park. Known for breathtaking sunsets over the Main Harbour, live music under the open sky, and a relaxed summer atmosphere that draws locals and visitors alike.

The covered stage hosts live acts throughout the season while the wide terrace gives you front-row seats to one of the finest views in the city. Affordable drinks, friendly staff and a charming atmosphere — it is easy to see why Palski has earned its place as the heart of Kotka's summer social scene.

"One of the best places to enjoy summertime sunsets in Kotka."

From the rocky lookout you can see the Vellamo Maritime Museum, the icebreaker Tarmo, and the Sunila industrial area designed by Alvar Aalto — all from your bar stool.

Location

Find Palski

Palski sits at the top of Palotorninvuori hill inside Palotorninvuori park, with a commanding view over Kotka's Main Harbour. The address is Palotorninvuori, 48100 Kotka.

The walk up the hill is part of the experience — you arrive at a natural rocky viewpoint that doubles as one of the most atmospheric outdoor bars in Finland.

Getting There

On foot: a short walk uphill through Palotorninvuori park from central Kotka
By car: park at the base of Palotorninvuori and walk up to the bar
Landmarks nearby: the Vellamo Maritime Museum and the Kotka waterfront are within easy reach
