Our Story

A genuine taste of Korea in Porvoo

Kimchi Korean Kitchen is the only Korean restaurant in Porvoo — a rare find in this historic Finnish city. We serve authentic Korean cuisine from scratch: rich bibimbap bowls, marinated bulgogi, soulful kimchi stews, and much more.

Our menu spans over 60 dishes across starters, soups, noodles, main courses and desserts. We cater to meat-lovers, vegetarians and vegans alike. Every dish is made to order with care, in the Korean spirit of honest, flavourful cooking.

Stop in for a leisurely dinner or join our popular lunch buffet (Tuesday–Thursday, 11:00–15:30). Small restaurant, big flavours — come as you are.
