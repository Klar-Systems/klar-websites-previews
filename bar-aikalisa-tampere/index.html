Our Story
The Heart of Janka, Tampere

Tampere's Legendary Neighbourhood Sports Bar

Bar Aikalisä has been the go-to spot for Janka locals for decades. Whether it's an Ilves–Tappara derby, a Formula 1 race, or a Friday night with live music, Aikalisä is where Tampere gathers.

The bar shows SM-liiga, Valioliiga, Formula 1, skiing, tennis, volleyball, and basketball on large LED screens. Every Saturday there is karaoke, and live bands take the stage regularly throughout the week. Monday and Wednesday evenings feature pub quizzes — Bingo nights run on Thursdays.

Food comes from Roseanne Pizzeria operating inside the bar: classic pizzas, wings, and kebab served straight to your table at prices that won't break the bank.

Events & Programme

Something On Every Night

Live music, karaoke, pub quizzes, bingo — and all major sports on big screens. Get in touch about private events or group bookings.

Sports on Big Screens

SM-liiga (Ilves & Tappara), Valioliiga, Formula 1, skiing, tennis, volleyball and basketball — broadcast live every week. The best place in Janka to watch the game with a cold drink and a pizza.

Live Music & Karaoke

Live bands perform regularly on weekends. Karaoke every Saturday. If you want a lively Friday night with music and a crowd, this is the place.

Pub Quiz & Bingo

Sports and music quiz on Mondays and Wednesdays. Bingo on Thursdays. Regulars welcome — come alone or bring a team.
