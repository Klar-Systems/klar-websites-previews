Story

A living room on Mikonkatu.

99 TopMeal doesn't perform authenticity — it just happens to be authentic. Nanchang noodle traditions, house broths, and owner Chunyan Tang at the counter telling you where each bowl comes from.

"this was meant to be a living room for everyone in Helsinki"— the owner, quoted in Danielle Burns' review

The restaurant opened in autumn 2023. Two years and 1 600+ Google reviews later, it's one of the highest-rated noodle houses in the city.
