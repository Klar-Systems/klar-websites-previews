About

The buffet Lahti keeps coming back to

With over 1,000 Google reviews and a 4.3-star rating, Sushi Royal has earned its place as one of the most popular Asian restaurants in Lahti.

The concept is simple: an all-you-can-eat buffet that spans Chinese, Japanese, and Thai cuisine. Sushi, stir-fry, noodles, soups, spring rolls, curries — all under one roof, all at a price that keeps regulars coming back for lunch and dinner.

Customers praise the variety and value. The staff is consistently described as friendly and fast.
