Our Story
Est. 1993 — Hamina's Own Pub

Everything You Want from a Pub

Jack-Up — or simply Jaska, as locals call it — has been at the heart of Hamina since 1993. Over thirty years, it has become the go-to gathering place for the people of this coastal city: a proper pub with a full licence, a wide selection of beers, free Wi-Fi, and something to eat.

In summer, the terrace opens up and the city comes outside. Throughout the year, regular live music nights and themed events keep the calendar full. Whether it's a quiet drink on a Tuesday or a Friday night out, Jack-Up is ready.
