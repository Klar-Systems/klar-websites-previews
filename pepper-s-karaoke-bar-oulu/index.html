About Us

Oulu's Classic
Karaoke Bar

Pepper's is a charming local bar on Isokatu, right in the heart of Oulu. Open from 9 in the morning, seven days a week. #15 of 192 pubs and bars in Oulu · 3.7 / 5 on Google.

Whether you're a karaoke veteran or a first-timer, you'll find a welcoming crowd and a well-stocked bar. Filet americain, light snacks, and all the drinks you need for a good evening.
