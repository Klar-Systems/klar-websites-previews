Ysitien Grilli has been serving travellers and locals on Highway 9 east of Jyväskylä since 2005. A straightforward roadside stop — good food, honest prices, no frills.

Whether you are heading toward Kuopio or just passing through Lievestuore, it is a reliable place to stop when hunger strikes.
