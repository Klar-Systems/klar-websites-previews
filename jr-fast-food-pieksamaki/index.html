About

Station-side since the '80s

JR Fast Food has been grilling on Myllykatu for decades — 87 metres from Pieksämäki railway station. A classic Finnish grill kiosk: burgers, fries, kebab meals, cold drinks, no fuss.

Locals know it for the cheese burgers and the late Friday and Saturday hours. Travellers know it as the place you can reach in under two minutes after stepping off the train.

Local quirk The Elvis-themed restroom was installed in 1997 to mark twenty years since Elvis Presley's passing. It has been covered by Helsingin Sanomat and Yle.
