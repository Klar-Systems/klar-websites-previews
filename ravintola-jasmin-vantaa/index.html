About

Family recipes, generous portions

With over 800 Google reviews and a 4.6-star rating, Ravintola Jasmin has become one of Myyrmaki's most loved restaurants since opening on Norotie.

The menu spans authentic Syrian and Yemeni cuisine: slow-cooked mandi, fragrant kabsa, hand-rolled shawarma, and traditional starters like hummus, baba ghanoosh, and tabbouleh. Every dish is made from family recipes with over 20 years of culinary tradition behind them.

Customers keep coming back for the generous portions, the warm hospitality, and a small detail that says everything about how this family runs their restaurant: complimentary soup and tea with every dine-in order.
