About Us

Pirkanmaa's Wing Legend

Sippuran Siipibaari has been serving the best chicken wings in Ylöjärvi since 2009. Born in Soppeenmäki, we've spent over 15 years perfecting the art of the hot wing — with authentic American-style recipes and a passion for quality that keeps locals coming back.

Our chicken is sourced entirely from Finland, ensuring freshness and supporting local farming. We toss our wings in the legendary Siipiweikot sauces — from mild BBQ to face-melting X-hot — so every bite delivers exactly the heat level you're after.

Join us at the bar, grab a cold one with your A-licence drinks, and settle in for some wings. Summer? Our terrace is waiting. Bringing a group? You're in the right place.
