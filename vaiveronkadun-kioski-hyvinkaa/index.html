The Legend

Vaiveronkadun Kioski has stood on Vaiveronkatu since 1988 — run today by brothers Sebastian and Mikael Mustajoki. It is not just a kiosk. It is the grill that draws customers from Espoo, Vantaa and Nurmijärvi on a Sunday afternoon, and can serve over 300 portions in six hours without missing a beat.

Quality grill food closer to Finnish tradition than international street food — that is the line reviewers keep coming back to. No gimmicks. Four burger patty sizes. One secret weapon.

The secret spice blend — kept under lock and key, described by Aamuposti as "just as secret as the Coca-Cola recipe." It goes into every burger at Vaiveronkadun Kioski. You can taste it. You will not replicate it.
