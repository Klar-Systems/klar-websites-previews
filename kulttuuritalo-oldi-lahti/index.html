About Us

Kulttuuritalo Oldi is a charming establishment located in Lahti's cultural quarter, featuring a warm, rustic atmosphere with a modern edge.

With a selection of local craft beers, creative cocktails, and food available throughout the day, Oldi is the perfect spot for breakfast, lunch, dinner, or evening drinks. Our pinball machines and view of the square make it a true local favorite.
