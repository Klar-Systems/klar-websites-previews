Our Story

Kouvola's favourite late-night pub

Bar Q has been a cornerstone of Kouvola's nightlife for years. Located on Kouvolankatu 30 in the heart of the city, it's the place locals go when they want a proper night out — whether that means shooting pool, belting out a karaoke favourite, or catching live music with friends.

With over 300 Google reviews and a loyal regular crowd, Bar Q is more than a pub — it's a Kouvola institution. Open until the early hours, seven nights a week.

"#7 of 95 pubs and bars in Kouvola" — rated by the people who know their local scene.
What's On

Something happening every night

From pool tournaments to karaoke nights and live bands, Bar Q keeps Kouvola entertained. Here is what you can expect when you walk through the door.

Live Music

Live bands and performers take the stage at Bar Q on a regular basis. Check our Facebook page for upcoming gig dates and get there early to grab a good spot.

Karaoke

Kouvola's best karaoke nights happen right here. Grab the mic, pick your song, and let the crowd cheer you on. No experience needed — just enthusiasm.

Pool Tables

Test your skills on the pool table. Challenge your mates to a match, or join in when someone needs a partner. The tables are always busy — arrive ready to play.

Darts

Bar Q has darts for those who like their competition a little more precise. Perfect for a friendly evening competition or a serious league match.

Private Parties & Events

Planning a celebration, work party, or pikkujoulut gathering? Bar Q is the natural venue choice in Kouvola. Call us to discuss your event.

Late Night Crowd

When other bars close, Bar Q is still going strong. Open until 3 AM on weekdays and 4:30 AM on Fridays and Saturdays — Kouvola's last stop and best one.
