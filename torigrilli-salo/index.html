About

A Salo grill kiosk, run by people who show up.

Torigrilli sits on Horninkatu in central Salo, a few steps from Salon VPK:n Talo. A Vietnamese-Finnish family runs the counter, and the crowd is mostly locals — lunch regulars, late-night finishers, and the Friday–Saturday stretch that closes at 5 AM.

Customers keep mentioning one thing: the staff actually cares. One regular wrote in publicly to thank the crew for carrying orders to their car in winter cold and labelling each bag by name so nobody got the wrong order. That's the energy.

Pricing sits in the €5–€10 range per person. You can call, walk up to the counter, or order through Wolt.
