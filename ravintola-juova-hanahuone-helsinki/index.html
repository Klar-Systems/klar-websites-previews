Our story

A shared taproom born from Finnish craft brewing

Juova Hanahuone started as a co-operation of Finnish microbreweries — a single place where locals could discover the full breadth of what Finland's craft beer scene had to offer. The idea was simple: bring the breweries to one address, pour from the source, and keep it real.

Today, the taproom features 25 rotating taps from breweries including Olarin Panimo, ETKO, Tuju, Mallassepät, CoolHead, Pien, Maistila and Tired Uncle — plus hundreds more in cans and bottles. Board games, a curated whiskey selection, wines, ciders, and a cozy interior with bold brewery-branded areas round out the experience.

We're on the ground floor of Kaisaniemenkatu 3, a two-minute walk from Helsinki Central Railway Station and right next to the Kaisaniemi metro stop.

Events

More than a bar

Juova Hanahuone is a regular venue for brewery showcases, tap takeovers, seasonal festivals and Helsinki Beer Week events. Follow us on Facebook or Instagram for the current schedule.

Annual festival

Lagerfest!

Our flagship autumn festival — seasonal and classic lagers from Finland and worldwide. Poured in generous 1-litre portions. DJ Xybo on Saturday evenings.

Brewery showcase

ETKO Showcase

Full tap takeovers featuring new ETKO releases and collaborations. One of Helsinki's best meet-the-brewer events.

Beer week

Helsinki Beer Week

Juova Hanahuone is a regular host venue for Helsinki Beer Week — international showcases, rare tappings and collaborative releases.

Tap takeover

Meet the Brewer

Regular evenings where participating breweries take over the taps and the brewers themselves come in to pour and talk about their beers.

Private events & group bookings

Want to host a private event, organise a group visit or discuss a brewery showcase? Get in touch — we'd be happy to talk.
