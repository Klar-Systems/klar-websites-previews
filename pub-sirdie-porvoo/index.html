Our story

A Kallio legend since 1966.

Pub Sirdie is a small bar in the middle of Kallio. Founded in 1966, in a neighbourhood that was considered shady and restless in those days. The bar wants to stay true to its original roots and maintains the same kind of atmosphere that Kallio had back in the days.

The jukebox still holds old records from the 60s and 70s — €1 for one song, €2 for four. Drinks are true to Finnish tradition. Parts of Aki Kaurismäki's Calamari Union were filmed right here.

In 2018, a fundraising effort by Kallio activists allowed the Friends of Sirdie collective to purchase the bar. Today it is owned by over 1,000 people — a real community pub, run by the community.
