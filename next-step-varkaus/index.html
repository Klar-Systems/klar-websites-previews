A Varkaus institution

Next Step is Varkaus's go-to sports bar — pool tables, pinball, big-screen sports, and karaoke nights that fill every weekend. We've been the meeting point for locals and visitors alike for over a decade.

Three hundred and fifty-three reviews on Google, a steady 3.8-star average, and a regulars' table that's never empty for long. That's the room you're walking into.

What’s on

Karaoke is our heartbeat. Live sports broadcasts, themed nights, and weekend DJs round out the calendar. Drop by for the regular slate or watch the door for special evenings — every week we run something a little different.

Karaoke nights

The mic is open most evenings. Sing solo or grab the table next to yours and turn it into a duet.

Live sports

Big-screen broadcasts of Liiga, Premier League, F1, and major tournaments. Cheer with the crowd, not your phone.

Weekend DJ & theme nights

Friday and Saturday late-night DJ sets and the occasional theme evening — follow us on Instagram for the next one.
