Views that inspired a national poem — and a café at the foot of the tower.

Where the landscape
meets a summer café

Haralanharjun näkötorni stands on a ridge in Suinula, Kangasala — a vantage point so remarkable that it moved Zachris Topelius to write the poem Kesäpäivä Kangasalla, later set to music as the regional anthem of Pirkanmaa.

At the base of the tower, the summer café run by Suinulan Nuorisoseura serves homemade pastries, refreshments, and local ice cream. A performance stage on the grounds hosts live music and theatre throughout the season.

Events & summer programme

The grounds around the tower come alive each summer with cultural performances. The stage area hosts live music, theatre, and community events throughout the season.

Live music

Seasonal performances on the outdoor stage. Programme announced on the Facebook page each summer.

Summer theatre

Theatre performances are staged in the natural setting of Haralanharju during the summer season.
